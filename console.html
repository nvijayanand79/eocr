Close this job

A job closes by itself when ACE calls back. Close it by hand only when the callback never comes: the console first asks ACE's Status API; if ACE has finished it retrieves the result (reconciliation), otherwise the job is recorded as abandoned.

How the eOCR simulator works

  

    
The console plays eOCR in the ACE to eOCR integration (spec v1.1). A job is one eOCR execution for one loan.

    
Enter the loan details (they go into the control file)
Add the documents: they upload into the job's S3 folder

      
Review the control file, generated from your loan details and documents
Check the S3 folder and submit the job to ACE

      
Follow ACE's stages; the job closes when ACE calls back, and the result is checked

    

      
Job (correlationId)
One eOCR run, stored under loanId=…/correlationId=…/ in the intake bucket.

      
ACE job (aceJobId)
The number ACE gives the job when it accepts it. Every status and callback refers to it.

      
Control file
JSON next to the documents: the loan data, the document list and whether ACE should extract fields.

      
Stages
eOCR steps (blue) and the stages ACE reports through its Status API (purple): pre-check, classification, extraction, validation…

      
HITL review
ACE paused for a person to confirm loan data it could not match on the NOTE.

      
Callback
ACE calling eOCR back with the final outcome; the job closes on it.

      
Outcomes
0 Completed · 1000 Pre-check failed (bad or unreadable files) · 2000 Validation failed (loan data ≠ NOTE) · 3000 Processing failed (ACE error).

      
Where the data comes from
Everything about progress and results comes from ACE (Status API, callback, output bucket). The console records and checks it.
